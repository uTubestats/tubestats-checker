Check the public numbers on any YouTube video

Paste a link to a public video or Short. You get the title, channel, publish date, views, likes and comments, plus the like-to-view ratio, straight from the official YouTube Data API.

Works with youtube.com/watch, youtu.be and youtube.com/shorts links.

What you will and will not see

This tool reads only public data. Likes and comment counts can be hidden by creators, and numbers may lag behind what the YouTube app shows. Watch time, impressions, revenue and audience data are private to the channel owner and are never available here. When a number is missing, the page says so instead of guessing.
